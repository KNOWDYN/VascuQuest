03 / virtual cardiovascular cohorts

06 / scientific provenance

Every vascular result retains its scientific identity.

Source physiology, reconstructed quantities, modelled disease and derived mechanics are not collapsed into one undifferentiated output. Evidence class, method identity and provenance remain explicit.

Medical relevance without medical overclaim.

VascuQuest is research software. Its disease states are mechanistic and MODELLED. A virtual subject is a simulation instance, not a patient. The platform is not represented as a clinical diagnostic system, patient digital twin, device-performance predictor or regulatory-qualified evidence generator.

No patient digital-twin claim
No device-deployment simulation
No clinical diagnosis / prognosis
No regulatory qualification claim
